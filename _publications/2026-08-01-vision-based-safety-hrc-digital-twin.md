### Summary

This work presents a low-cost, vision-based safety monitoring system for human–robot collaboration (HRC) in industrial environments. A single overhead RGB camera, combined with a YOLOv8 instance-segmentation model, detects human operators in the shared workspace. The workspace is divided into green, yellow and red safety zones according to the operator's position. The red zone is derived from the robot's reachable workspace in RoboDK and triggers a functional stop when it is entered. A real-time digital twin of the robot in RoboDK provides synchronized monitoring of robot motion, enabling adaptive safety behavior without dedicated industrial safety sensors.
